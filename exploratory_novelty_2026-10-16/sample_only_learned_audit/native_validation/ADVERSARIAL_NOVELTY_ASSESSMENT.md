# Adversarial novelty assessment: native sample-trajectory audit

16 October 2026. Primary-source review through this date. Read-only with respect to all experiment sources, plans, outcomes and manuscript prose. No native endpoint was inspected, no experiment was run, and no numerical choice was changed. The only separately authorized manuscript change was adding `budde2025soundsmc` to `references.bib`.

## Verdict

**No exact prior paper matching the complete ordered financial-event certificate was identified. This does not establish a first-in-literature claim.** Its strongest defensible contribution is an accounting-specific engineering verification construction: attribute forgiveness at the native event boundary, preserve actual adaptive state evolution through unrelated flows, derive deterministic supports, and confirm a declared financial-component property for executable archived RL controllers.

The proposed two-sign control, conditional averaging, martingale centering, independent selection/confirmation and lower confidence inference are established ingredients. Their assembly here does not support a strong new generic AI/statistical algorithm claim. Whether learned nuisance models add practical value remains an empirical question; no outcome from the running comparison is used in this assessment.

## What the accounting theorem actually adds

For a finite ordered sequence of financial events, let gross flow be conditionally mean zero, executed costs be nonnegative with known discounted total bound `Cbar`, and injections be the native financial-event injections at actual pre-event cash. Nonfinancial flows may alter subsequent states, but their direct flows/injections are excluded from the target. Known fair signs permit local averaging `A=sum w(Iplus+Iminus)/2`, preserving the expected charged component under the actual adaptive policy. A predictable control with absolute path budget `B` remains centered for any finite frozen predictor. Hence

`Z=(1-alpha)A-C-M`, `E Z=J_financial`, `Z>=-Cbar-B`, and `min(Z,b)` has mean at most `J_financial` and a known bounded range.

This is useful because it states exactly which cash changes count, which alternative local event may be evaluated, and what information must precede the coin. Native fee/maturity ordering cannot be replaced by netting and clamping; physical/battery recapitalization cannot be called financial forgiveness. A learned transition model need not be correct because both coefficient branches use the same predictable model. These are concrete software-contract/proof obligations, rather than a new neural estimator principle. The software is instrumented and checked; the current numerical allowance is not a formal floating-point verification theorem.

Two qualifications materially limit the novelty:

1. **The realized-injection residual already removes the full moment oracle.** `Z_real=(1-alpha)sum w I-C` has the same expected component and lower endpoint `-Cbar` under mean-zero gross, without sign symmetry or a fitted critic. It is already a baseline in this manuscript. Pairing does not create that negative endpoint. Its extra ingredient is local sign integration; for separate pure-maturity events with a known gross envelope, it sharpens the injection upper envelope from `Gbar` to `Gbar/2`. Oracle reduction is relative to the stronger exact-kernel estimator, not the first sample-only certificate even within this project.
2. **Local pairing is not whole-trajectory Rao–Blackwellization.** Actual cash, later actions and later costs depend on earlier realized signs. A sum of locally averaged injections need not be a conditional expectation of the entire raw return given sign-free inputs. No trajectory-variance or confirmation-power dominance follows.

## Additional sound-verification antecedent and precise distinction

Budde, Hartmanns, Meggendorfer, Weininger and Wienhöft, *Sound Statistical Model Checking for Probabilities and Expected Rewards*, TACAS 2025, LNCS 15696:167–190, DOI `10.1007/978-3-031-90643-5_9`. [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-90643-5_9); [primary full text](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-031-90643-5_9.pdf).

Section 4.2, Definition 3 and Theorem 1, printed p.179 (PDF page 13/24), give DKW-E-Lower: finite-sample sound lower coverage for nonnegative finite-mean path rewards, with distribution-dependent eventual closeness. The following paragraph extends it to a known lower endpoint by shifting. It supplies neither a prescribed finite-N precision/power guarantee nor the financial endpoint. Our implementation instead upper-caps a cost-lower-bounded subtarget and uses fixed-N empirical Bernstein. These are different procedures; the new source blocks generic novelty for support-based expected-reward verification, not the accounting identity. DKW is a plausible future competitor, with no asserted power advantage and no late addition to the immutable current comparison.

## Exact-match search and defensible wording

Targeted searches covered limited liability/RL, capital injections/RL, capital injections with Rao–Blackwellization or variance reduction, financial reward-hacking verification, accounting/martingale reward auditing, and sound expected-reward model checking, including available 2026 primary records. The sources above contain exact matches for individual statistical ingredients and close financial/verification neighbors. No reviewed source states the full actual-state, ordered-financial-event construction with interleaved nonfinancial attribution, arbitrary predictable budgeted controls and a charged-positive/booked-negative component certificate. This is a bounded search result, not proof that the combination is unprecedented.

Suggested related-work/proof addition using `budde2025soundsmc`:

> Sound statistical model checking already provides lower confidence guarantees for path rewards with known lower support. Our accounting construction supplies the financial-component endpoint; independent capped confirmation uses established machinery rather than a new general inference theorem.

Suggested contribution wording:

> We construct an instrumented financial-event audit for executable archived RL controllers. Ordered local ledger updates and explicit cost/control budgets give a valid one-sided component-return certificate under known conditional sign symmetry, while preserving native adaptive state evolution and excluding unrelated recapitalization.

Avoid “first sample-only certificate,” “new neural verification algorithm,” “oracle free,” or “Rao–Blackwellization guarantees variance reduction.” A more exact phrase is **“without conditional-magnitude integration, using known sign probabilities and trusted local ledger access.”**

## What can make the engineering contribution compelling

Report whether native event ordering/component attribution prevents concrete false certificates, and whether learned controls improve confirmation beyond the already valid realized and paired residuals after model-query/fit overhead. Retain every abstention and known-null false positive. Twenty archived actors crossed with two profiles are not forty newly trained policies; inherited claims cannot establish acquisition of an exploit after intervention. If simple pairing ties the learned controls, claim the audited financial software contract and accounting application, not demonstrated AI-method superiority. No wording change can substitute for that empirical distinction.

### Ranked source wording findings

1. **High scientific-positioning priority:** `sections/introduction.tex:10` and `sections/conclusion.tex:7` foreground avoiding moment integration, whereas `sections/predictable_audit.tex:3` and `sections/discussion.tex:8` correctly concede that the simpler realized residual already does this. Lead with the accounting target/contract, then compare representations. Suggested contribution opening: “We construct an instrumented financial-event audit for executable learned controllers, preserving native event ordering and actual-state feedback while excluding unrelated recapitalization. A realized-injection residual supplies a baseline certificate under a deterministic cost envelope; local pairing and budgeted fitted controls are evaluated for confirmation efficiency.” Retain the explicit symmetry, attribution and cost assumptions for the paired/learned extensions. A tie cannot establish that supervised controls are needed.

2. **High interpretation priority:** `sections/predictable_audit.tex:12,38` should state plainly that the native stress test injects fresh fair maturity signs under the declared simulator intervention. The mathematical assumption is clear, but “actual learned policies” can otherwise suggest a certificate under their original market law. Suggested clause: “We impose fresh, hidden fair maturity signs while retaining the declared magnitudes and native state evolution.” `sections/introduction.tex:5` could qualify the audit question as positive charged and negative booked **financial-component** means; the new native target excludes physical returns and inherited forgiveness does not prove new exploit acquisition.

3. **Medium access/novelty priority:** `sections/related_work.tex:12` says conditional-magnitude integration “is replaced” by sign access and local ledger queries. These are reduced requirements relative to the exact-kernel variant, but additional requirements relative to the realized residual. 4. **Minor mathematical drafting correction:** `sections/predictable_audit.tex:5,10` and `sections/supp_sample_only_audit.tex:10,23` allow arbitrary nonnegative predictable weights but state integrability for gross flows/injections rather than their weighted sums. Say “assume integrability of the weighted gross flows and injections,” or explicitly restrict finite-horizon discount weights to a deterministic bound. Native ordinary discounting satisfies the latter; this is a theorem-statement precision issue, not an observed native failure.

5. **Structure:** retain the independent main Section 4 before the engine case and keep acquisition separate. Make the access ladder visible in one sentence: realized residual → extra local-sign access → optional independently fitted control; stronger exact-kernel repair is a different access model. The supplement's access table already gives this accurately. Once results are released, synchronize the abstract, which currently still leads entirely with the older oracle variant (`main.tex:44`), rather than silently discarding its retained positive and negative evidence.

### Official journal scope and two concrete precedents

The [official Elsevier description](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/engineering-applications-of-artificial-intelligence/0952-1976) asks for novel aspects of AI in an engineering application and replicable validation. It explicitly includes verification/validation of AI-based software, diagnostics and industrial case studies, and asks the abstract to distinguish the AI contribution from its engineering application. It does **not** state that every submission must invent a general neural architecture or learning algorithm.

Two primary EAAI examples support that distinction:

- Rossi, Bernardeschi and Cococcioni, [*Neural networks in closed-loop systems: Verification using interval arithmetic and formal prover*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2024.109238), 137B:109238 (2024), combine established verification tools for learned controllers and compare verification capability/timing. The [authors' institutional record](https://arpi.unipi.it/handle/11568/1260707) provides the primary abstract and published full text. This is a close AI-software-verification positioning precedent, not an accounting-estimator antecedent.
- Rochefort-Beaudoin et al., [*Structural design through reinforcement learning*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.112273), 161:112273 (2025), contribute an open engineering-specific RL environment, physics-based reward and observation interface, evaluated with established PPO/Dreamer agents. The [DTU primary record](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/structural-design-through-reinforcement-learning/) supplies the full published paper. It illustrates that environment/contract construction can carry application novelty when the demonstrated capability is concrete.

These cases support journal **fit**, not acceptance or proof of this manuscript's novelty. A defensible AI contribution is an executable accounting-validation procedure for learned decision systems, with explicit access/support obligations and demonstrated prevention or certification of a specified implementation failure. Its practical value must come from verified native behavior, attribution checks, or confirmation efficiency beyond the simplest residual; relabeling classical controls as AI novelty would weaken it. Neither example needs adding to the manuscript bibliography solely to establish venue fit.
