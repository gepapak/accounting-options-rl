The proposal is valid as a separately frozen exploratory estimand before any
confirmation draw. Audit-created maturity gross is still a sum of predictable
exposures times fresh fair signs. Earlier excluded signs can affect later states
and actions, but the next fresh sign remains conditionally centered. Consequently
E[G_new]=0 and E[booked_new]=-E[C]. Local sign-paired injections are unbiased at
the actual state. Any primary predictor, centered with the same model on both
branches and the pre-coin absolute-amplitude budget, remains a zero-mean control;
its predictive accuracy for the changed target is unnecessary for validity.

The old-event identification is justified only by the fixed six-step source
contract and initial-claim metadata. The analyzer must check both. A time mask
without that source check would be insufficient in a variable-lag engine.
Retaining costs and fee injections preserves conservative -Cbar residual and
-Cbar-B control lower bounds. The primary gross bound covers all retained gross,
so the original raw and positive-side supports remain conservative.

auxiliary proposal arose after development generation began. The unchanged
primary selection rule sees no confirmation data. Its choices may be inefficient
for the new target, which must be reported. A separate .05/240 allocation covers
all forty cases and six methods without assuming independence across methods or
cases. Analytic mandatory cost harm requires no additional probability event.

The narrower target does NOT remove old claims from dynamics. Old cash, floor,
margin and later policy effects remain. It is not a new-versus-inherited causal
contrast, counterfactual flat advantage, acquisition experiment or full lifecycle
return. Its 95% family is separate from the primary family; no joint95% claim.
Original and auxiliary failure/abstention rows must both remain visible.
