# Study EXT pre-registration: an unmodified public simulator

Written 6 November 2026, before any registered run of this study. Internal pre-registration: the files listed in `PREREGISTRATION_HASHES_EXT.txt` are hashed (SHA-256, UTC time) before launch. No public registry is claimed. Every run and every hypothesis is reported, whatever the result.

## Question and what precedes it
Every learning experiment that showed the exploit used sleeves written by the authors; the one public simulator tried, gym-mtsim (Study G), was inconclusive because its agents never opened a position. Gym-Trading-Env (ClementPerroud, commit a86fc03) is an independent public simulator whose source the authors did not write. On a step that takes the portfolio's valuation to zero or below it ends the episode and does not call the reward function, so the whole loss of that step goes unrewarded. Its public wealth keeps the negative value, so the direct calculation (the change of public wealth) is available here and is the ledger arm: this study cannot show that a twin is needed in this simulator. It asks (1) whether PPO acquires the exploit in code the authors did not write, (2) whether the remedies carry over, and (3) whether the reward diagram reads this accounting from the reward alone, which is a different shape from a floor.

**Development (seen before this file).** The engine's source and a scripted test (`scripted_ext.py`, `scripted_ext2.py`; no learning). Constant position in the placebo market over 3,000 episodes: at position 10 the reward totals +35.5% of initial wealth per 30-day episode and the booked change about 0 (bust in 50% of episodes); at position 5, +7 to +13%; at positions up to 2, about 0. In the edge market the booked return also rises with leverage (+272% at 10 against +94% at 5, signal accuracy 0.6): with a risk-neutral linear reward the honest policy also wants maximal leverage, so an edge market cannot separate the exploit from honest trading here, and this study uses the placebo market only. Smoke tests (20,000 steps, seed 300, `smoke_results/`, excluded). A pilot at 10^6 steps, seed 99 (`pilot_results/`), arms REP, LEDGER and FINDER: REP chose position 10 at 61% of the probes, went bust in 60% of the test windows and reported +145% against +72% booked; LEDGER 1% of the probes, no bust, -1.8%. The seeds below are fresh, but the pilot informed the budget and the arms. Earlier work by an AI assistant (`_archive/.../external_gym_trading_learning_001`, one seed, 32,768 steps, real signs of the same file, found no material fault activation at that budget) is the only earlier training in this simulator; it did not use a placebo market.

## Design (`envs_ext.py`, `run_ext.py`, `scan_ext.py`, `aggregate_ext.py`)
- **Simulator.** The official `TradingEnv`, byte-identical to the pinned commit (checked at import), driven through its constructor, its data feed and its documented custom reward: the change of portfolio valuation, percent of the initial wealth. Initial wealth 1,000, trading fee 1 bp, no borrowing interest (its known issue #23 is thereby isolated), 30-step episodes, initial position 0. Position set: `[-10, -5, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 5, 10]`, the range of the tutorial's leverage discussion; the documented default `[0, 1]` for the control.
- **Market.** Daily moves of BTC/USD from the engine's official example file (complete UTC days; magnitudes up to 2019 for training, from 2020 for testing), each with a fresh fair sign in every episode, and an independent signal `z`. No policy can profit in expectation. The file's licence is not stated, so it is fetched by hash and not redistributed.
- **Observation.** `z`, wealth over initial wealth, requested position over 10, share of the episode left.
- **Learner.** PPO as in Studies T to Z: 8 environments, n_steps 1024, batch 256, gamma 0.99, 64x64, reward normalization, 10^6 steps.
- **Arms.** REP (the engine's reward); LEDGER (change of public wealth); SPR and TIR (one clone with the next price reflected about the current one; own cost from the reward at no move on a second clone); TSR (four more clones far along the line); RUIN5, RUIN25, RUIN100 (a lump-sum penalty at a bust, percent of initial wealth, on the engine's reward: the strengths of the earlier studies and one set from the scripted test's size of the credit); FINDER (reward = the bracket's width); FINDER_DEF (the same in the default position set, the negative control).
- **Seeds.** 300-307 per arm; TSR 300-303, since where it is exact its reward equals the ledger's; FINDER_DEF 300-301 at 10^5 steps, since its reward should be zero everywhere. 62 runs. A missing or failed run is reported and not replaced.
- **Measures per run (greedy policy, `model.predict`).** Exploit share `X`: the share of 90 probes (wealth 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4; `z` = -1, +1; share left 0.2, 0.5, 0.9; requested position -10, 0, 10 over 10) at which the policy requests position +-10. On 100 fixed test windows of the placebo market (magnitudes from 2020 on): reported and booked return, percent of initial wealth per episode; bust share; mean absolute position; the bracket's width; the ledger-free estimates of the booked return from SPR, TIR and the tail reading, with the bracket checked at every step.
- **Part S, the diagram (`scan_ext.py`; no learning).** 4,000 (state, action) pairs along random-policy episodes in each position set. Tail reading of the booked change; search for where the reward leaves its affine piece; the jump's location and size against the truth from the public wealth, which the reading does not see.

## Hypotheses and decision rules (`aggregate_ext.py`)
"Present": `X(arm) > X(LEDGER)` by a one-sided Mann-Whitney test, `p < 0.05`, and `mean X(arm) >= mean X(LEDGER) + 0.10`. "Removes": `X(arm) < X(REP)` by a one-sided Mann-Whitney test, `p < 0.05`, and `mean X(arm) <= mean X(LEDGER) + 0.10`.
- **EA1.** PPO acquires the exploit in the unmodified simulator: REP present.
- **EA2.** The evaluation manufactures profit: for REP, mean reported minus mean booked return is at least 10 points, over the runs.
- **EA3.** The exploit ruins: the bust share of REP exceeds LEDGER's (one-sided Mann-Whitney, `p < 0.05`) by at least 0.10.
- **EA4, EA5, EA6.** SPR, TIR and TSR each remove the exploit (three decisions; for TSR four seeds).
- **EA7, EA8.** A ruin penalty of 5 and of 25 does not remove it: RUIN5 present, RUIN25 present.
- **EA9.** A ruin penalty of 100 removes it.
- **EA10.** The finder finds it: `X` at least 0.5 in at least 7 of the 8 FINDER runs.
- **EA11.** The finder's negative control: in the default position set the width over all test windows is at most 1e-6 points in both FINDER_DEF runs.
- **EA12.** The bracket contains the booked change at every audited step of all 62 runs.
- **EA13.** The tail reading's audit of the booked return errs by at most 0.01 points in every test window of every run.
- **EA14.** On the gambling agents SPR's audit fails and TIR's does not: over the REP runs, the mean absolute error of the SPR audit is at least 5 points, and that of the TIR audit at most 0.01 points.
- **ES1.** The tail reading is within 1e-6 points of the booked change on at least 99.9% of the states in each position set.
- **ES2.** Reward-only reading of the accounting: in the leverage set, of the states with a jump, at least 99% have it at the true location (within 1e-4 relative) and at least 99% have a size equal to the current wealth, within 5%.
- **ES3.** In the default position set the width at t = +1, -1 is zero at every state: nothing is forgiven at the realized moves, though the jump lies further along the line.
If EA1 fails, EA4-EA9 are reported as not testable. Seventeen decisions, each its own; no multiplicity correction.

*Prior belief (written before the full runs, after the pilot):* EA1 to EA3 probably hold (pilot 0.61 against 0.01), though some REP seeds may settle at position 5. EA4 to EA6 probably hold: under SPR, TIR and TSR leverage has no expected reward in a fair market. EA7 and EA8 probably hold, because the credit at position 10 is about 70 points per bust; RUIN25 is the less certain. EA9 is a guess: at 100 the expected penalty exceeds the credit at position 10, but position 5 may remain attractive. EA10 probably holds (a smoke run reached 0.91). EA11 to EA13 and ES1 to ES3 should hold by construction, and a failure would be an implementation error or a property of the engine that the source reading missed. EA14 holds if the favourable branch survives on bust steps, as it should.
