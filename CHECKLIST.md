# Evaluation checklist for RL trading agents and their simulators

For anyone who trains or accepts a learned trading controller on the basis of simulated performance, and for anyone who sets the settlement rules a trader operates under. Each item points to the evidence in this repository that shows why it matters.

## Accounting
- [ ] **Declare the liability rule.** State how the simulator books losses beyond equity, how positions are sized (fixed allocation or fraction of equity), and what happens at bankruptcy, alongside every reported result. A zero floor or a capped final loss is a modelling decision with behavioral consequences, not a safeguard.
  - Under a zero floor, agents learned to take maximal leverage near zero equity: 11.1 leverage units of 16 above full-liability agents (`LIABILITY_STUDY_2026-09-29/results_f/`).
- [ ] **Never floor cash silently; book the debt.** Either end the episode on ruin with the full loss booked, or book any recapitalization as debt in the reward and in the reported result. Real settlement systems require collateral precisely because a distressed party may stop paying.
  - Capping only the final loss taught far less gambling than a floor that keeps trading (Studies B, F, I, J).
- [ ] **Price the option your accounting writes.** Full liability, a capped final loss, a settlement lag, terminal limited liability and a zero floor form one ladder of increasing forgiveness. Under a settlement lag, insolvent but undetected agents took maximal leverage at every probe when their deficit would be forgiven (`ACCOUNTING_OPTIONS_2026-10-14/study_n/`).
- [ ] **Use a calibrated pre-training gap diagnostic where a reduced model is defensible.** The binned dynamic program gives an optimal-model benchmark. Its positive leverage-16 versus flat contrast is averaged over a fixed initial-equity reference grid relative to one-period noise, not actual training occupancy. O's previously fixed threshold classified 5/6 confident treatments; local gaps tied its prospective AUC. Calibration is specific to the learner, budget and model. At 90% charging, 0/10 PPO runs acquired an optimal exploit at one million steps, versus 8/10 at four million (`ACCOUNTING_OPTIONS_2026-10-14/study_o/`, `study_p/`). Neither a small index nor a short null run certifies safety.
- [ ] **If the account keeps trading after losses, charge every injection in full.** Under the no-edge assumptions this restores non-positive expected raw monetary reward. No fixed partial charge guarantees that uniformly across those markets; finite training and reward normalization remain separate issues. The matched-continuation contrast cut low-equity leverage by 9.6 units (`revision_experiments/2026-10-10/`).
- [ ] **If the ledger is not exposed, train on a sign-paired reward.** Clone the simulator before each step, step the clone with the sign of the exogenous price change reversed, and train on (r − r_twin)/2 − own cost. Check that the legitimate payoff is linear in that sign and that own costs are known. In Study S it removed the exploit and kept the ledger-trained edge (`ACCOUNTING_OPTIONS_2026-10-14/study_s/`). Do not rely on a KL penalty toward a safe policy: at every tested strength it either kept the exploit or lost over half the edge.
- [ ] **Separate universal charging from a kernel-specific repair bound.** For charges in `[0,1]`, a known finite uniform conditional ratio `h_t(0) <= rho*c_t` gives nonpositive expected floor/terminal reward for every admissible policy at `alpha >= 1-1/rho`, under no-edge gross payoffs. This is a declared-kernel guarantee; zero-cost positive-stop-loss actions defeat finite `rho`. Exact necessity additionally requires cash-independent laws and the offending action admissible at zero. The ratio is classical gain/loss mathematics, not evidence of future-market safety (`exploratory_novelty_2026-10-16/predictable_accounting_audit/THEORY.md`).
- [ ] **Reconcile reported equity with booked P&L** for every run, and report the largest gap (`tools/ledger_check.py`).
  - Choose a numerical tolerance appropriate to the ledger, frequency and currency units. The engine audit's registered reconciliation check flagged 53 of 54 affected runs and none of 246 others (`results/`); its threshold is not a universal accounting tolerance.
- [ ] **State whether position size scales with equity, and report ruin** (the share of runs whose booked equity reaches zero) alongside returns.

## Tests
- [ ] **Consider a cash-inflow endpoint for the uncharged component.** With nonnegative boundary cash and nonincreasing discounts, cash conservation bounds financial return below by minus initial cash and all excluded positive credits. Budget physical cash, refunds, conversions, unrelated recapitalizations and direct inherited maturity credits if those maturities are excluded from the target. Use source bounds, not observed maxima. A capped positive-component certificate alone needs no sign symmetry; strict booked harm needs its separate no-edge/cost proof. Partial charging needs an injection bound. Native floating-point allowances can retain stronger G/C access than the exact theorem (`native_validation/cash_inflow_budget/` under the sample-trajectory audit).
- [ ] **Give learned controls the same strong support contract as simple comparators.** A cash-funded RAW baseline and a cost-supported paired control have different negative endpoints. If adding the known current two-branch correction, compare against that analytic correction alone before attributing an improvement to the fitted continuation. Match the selected cap/beta/budget in descriptive zero-future and zero-control ablations, keep independent confirmation and disclose unequal selection-data/CPU budgets. A fitted predictor can worsen variance or select zero weight.
- [ ] **Use sample-trajectory controls only with valid structural centering.** Instrument ordered financial events, excluding nonfinancial flows and their injections. Pair local injections at actual pre-event cash under a known hidden conditionally fair sign; evaluate both hypothetical branches with the SAME independently fitted predictor. Debit a fixed absolute-amplitude control budget before reading the current sign, upper-cap after control subtraction, and freeze all choices before whole-episode confirmation. Predictive accuracy is not a validity assumption; conditional fairness and the executed-cost bound are. This is a specialization of established regression martingale controls (`exploratory_novelty_2026-10-16/sample_only_learned_audit/`).
- [ ] **Replay the authentic learned controller and cash-flow component.** Restore trained observations/actions, paired controllers and native history/normalization updates; freeze actor weights. Preserve maturity queues and report prepaid initial claims, interventions, fees and excluded transfers. Inspect engineered features as well as price histories: the native extension retains a full-scenario risk summary, so its fresh-sign certificate does not establish a wholly causal market backtest. A financial-component certificate is not total fund performance, an advantage over flat or new evidence that the actor learned an exploit.
- [ ] **Run a placebo market** (`LIABILITY_STUDY_2026-09-29/placebo_market.py`). Train and evaluate the agent on the same data with the sign of the payoff randomized independently in every period. No policy can profit there in expectation, so any positive expected reported return is a false positive.
  - Floor-trained agents reported +38% to +148% in the placebo market while booking −15% to −57% (`LIABILITY_STUDY_2026-09-29/results/`).
  - In the constant-position diffusion calculation with a zero start, five draws flag false profit about 90% of the time (Theorem 1 and Corollary 2 of the paper). An agent evaluated from a full allocation reaches the floor in only some draws: use enough draws to assess that incidence, and retain the trained agent as the unit of inference.
- [ ] **Probe the equity response.** Set the equity input of a trained agent to a low and a normal value and compare the risk it takes. The probe needs no access to the simulator's accounting; it is a screening signal, not proof. Report the exploit's shape (leverage at low minus at high equity), not only a count at one probe: where honest actions are nearly indifferent, the count alone has a seed-dependent floor.
- [ ] **Look for a feasible accounting-incentive witness before training.** In an independent fair-sign model, integrate the expected one-action reward and booked cost, and compare with staying flat under the same account. A positive proxy reward with negative expected booked profit proves an available incentive for that policy. The exact one-step and ledger/clock audits in `exploratory_novelty_2026-10-15/` supply examples; a failed witness does not certify safety, and a calibrated acquisition score is a separate heuristic. Conditional empirical precision does not establish future-market calibration. Study R's completed 60-agent cross-cost acquisition test failed its single primary composite (p=.8125), despite both full-forgiveness anchors being maximal in 10/10 runs. That failure does not invalidate the separate accounting-witness certificates.
- [ ] **Check adaptive continuations when isolated actions fail.** A negative prefix can reach a profitable low-ledger or clock state. Freeze an executable feedback policy and cap using discovery data, then confirm on independent IID whole paths. Under explicit no-edge assumptions, the accounting residual has a cost-only lower bound; a positive one-sided capped-residual bound plus strictly positive expected executed costs certifies charged gain with booked harm. Otherwise abstain. The selected-case 99% result in `exploratory_novelty_2026-10-15/adaptive_witness_empirical/` is policy/model-specific, not a benchmark discovery-rate or acquisition claim. Use the terminal deadline compiler only with its stationarity, no-wait-information and no-early-closure assumptions; do not transfer it to step-floor accounts.
- [ ] **Use predictable accounting drift only with valid conditional moment access.** Include every observation available before the controller acts. Exact stop-loss curves and known finite uniform mean-cost/stop-loss bounds permit uncapped bounded confirmation even with unbounded prices or realized fees. Finite moments alone, unconditional row means after current risk is revealed, and an uncalibrated fitted critic do not supply these guarantees. With finite `rho` and `kappa=(1-alpha)*rho-1>0`, a charged lower bound `ell>0` also proves `J_book <= -ell/kappa` on the same event, without a mandatory trade or separate noisy cost test. Check arithmetic/oracle error and charge-independent policy/dynamics before reusing a whole charge curve.
- [ ] **Test sensitivity to simulator assumptions.** Accept a learned controller only if its advantage over the rule survives the removal of each shortcut, and treat an advantage that appears only in markets without a real edge with particular suspicion (`GENERALIZATION_STUDY_2026-09-29/`).

## Market realism
- [ ] **Compare acquisition scores with cost-aware summaries on equal terms.** Include dose, executed cost relative to payoff noise, a dose/cost/risk ratio, and local or moment-based gaps. Calibrate on the same historical labels, report all equally optimal cutoff intervals, and evaluate budgets/learners separately. The completed-study comparison in `exploratory_novelty_2026-10-15/cost_baselines/` does not establish better prediction by the integrated diagnostic. A feasible accounting witness answers a different question: the matched-moment example shows that cost/variance/absolute-payoff summaries can conceal opposite one-action incentives. Do not promote that example to a learning prediction or safety certificate.
- [ ] **Model realistic trading costs.** Under Study H's tested fee, liquidity cap, price impact and training budget, the equity-only agents did not learn the predicted low-equity exploit (`LIABILITY_STUDY_2026-09-29/results_h/`). This does not show that frictions remove every accounting incentive.
- [ ] **Cap executed volume by executable market volume**, not by capital, and report the ratio of desired to executed volume.
  - Without a cap, one rule's return grew ×300–1,000 while a learned policy was ruined (engine audit).
- [ ] **Model solvency** (margin, loss exit or a budget constraint) for any account that can lose more than its capital.
  - A missing liquidity cap and missing solvency rules were jointly necessary for the engine audit's false positive.
- [ ] **Settle on the actual payoff** of the traded product and **respect information timing**: no interpolation between future prices, no normalization statistics fitted on test data, and gate-closure times that match the market.

- [ ] **Stress collateral components against exposure and the spread distribution under explicit assumptions.** A historical volume-term proxy was below a modeled three-week buffer in 36.8% of post-reform FI/SE/NO zone-months versus 8.6% before (`ACCOUNTING_OPTIONS_2026-10-14/`). The model assumes randomized signs, exposure equal to consumption and an external owner cost-of-risk hurdle. The invoice term, minima and monitoring are excluded; the historical formula applied after Finland's November 2024 change is counterfactual. Include current national rules and all collateral components before judging actual adequacy. The comparison identifies neither a causal reform effect nor real exploitation.

## Statistics and reporting
- [ ] **Select and rank on the booked ledger**, not on reported equity. No metric of reported equity identifies booked performance, and more evaluation draws do not repair a biased target: winners selected on reported return were certified worst in expected booked return (`revision_experiments/2026-10-10-selection/`).
- [ ] **Report every seed, and the median alongside the mean**, together with per-seed exposure and direction. Under zero-floor accounting the mean (+270%) and the interquartile mean (+73%) were fooled; the median (−101%) was not.
- [ ] **Annualize risk metrics at the decision frequency** and separate investor-level from fund-level metrics.
- [ ] **Pre-register protocol changes and report failed predictions.** Retain every gate of a composite endpoint. In completed R, the low-fee gap 4.500 passed >=4; the high-fee gap 4.475 failed <4; interaction .025 failed >=4; and the seed-cluster sign p=.8125 failed <=.05. Booked controls averaged 3.100/1.125 leverage, so a smaller raw treatment mean is not the required treatment-control interaction. The operational cutoffs do not prove population equivalence or no gambling (`ACCOUNTING_OPTIONS_2026-10-14/study_r/VERDICTS_R.md`).
- [ ] **Identify inference units and the status of each analysis.** Do not count evaluation draws or checkpoints as new trained agents. Retain matched seeds and zones in dependence sensitivity, disclose unadjusted hypothesis families, and label post-hoc logistic transitions and baseline comparisons exploratory. An interval containing an exponent establishes compatibility, not equivalence or a universal learning law.
- [ ] **Keep certificate selection and coverage explicit.** Distinguish discovery data, independent fixed-size confirmation and later algebraic charge/horizon families. Retain failed caps, negative cases and raw/clipped comparators; martingale subtraction need not reduce variance. Do not report opposite one-sided endpoints as a jointly guaranteed interval or combine separately allocated experiment families into paper-wide coverage. Infinite-variance synthetic validation establishes model capability, not future-market coverage.
- [ ] **Match simple multi-step policies and disclose oracle work.** Equal simulator-transition budgets do not equal CPU or conditional-integration budgets. The latest predictable audit has separate 720-, 45- and 32-bound families: better confirmation in the first two coexists with no extra held-out condition beyond exact isolated-action integration, successful simple delayed trading, a 3/16 tie on Pareto cases and variance increases in 4/16. These do not establish adaptive necessity or universal estimator dominance. Independent verification reports 552/552 checks; current build QA is reported in the companion manuscript README.
- [ ] **Keep hash-stamped records byte-exact.** Reproduce writers in a disposable copy, preserve append-only run-log prefixes, and run `python tools/verify_studies.py`. Independent statistics and completed-Q diagnostics in `exploratory_codex_2026-10-15/` read existing results without changing them. All six Q criteria were met, but DQN's fully charged controls often gambled; small treatment-control gaps are not evidence of no gambling. The exploratory DP-free rollout extension reports wide Monte Carlo uncertainty and transfer failures; it does not establish a general scalable predictor.
