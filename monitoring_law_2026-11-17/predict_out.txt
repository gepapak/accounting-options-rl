## D4. The paper's Nordic ladder (24 zone-years, placebo signs on real hourly spreads, from zero equity, fee-free)

For fixed magnitudes m_i with fair signs, the law gives ZF / TERM = 2 - 1.2533 kappa, with kappa = sum |m_i|^3 / (sum m_i^2)^1.5 (the constant is sqrt(2 pi) / 2, from the fixed-size-move overshoot 1/2). kappa is computed from the spreads; the measured ratios are recomputed with 20,000 sign draws.

Criterion: Spearman rank correlation of at least 0.8 between predicted and measured ratios, and a mean absolute error of at most 0.05.
