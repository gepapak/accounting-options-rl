# Repository description

## Repository name
`accounting-options-rl`

## GitHub "About" text (≤ 350 characters)
Reading hidden loss forgiveness from the reward: accounting rules are options that RL traders learn to exploit, and a simulator's reward along the market input is their payoff diagram. Theory with exact checks, 2,085 internally registered agents, an unmodified public simulator, real clones of an audited engine.

## Abstract

Reinforcement-learning (RL) traders for electricity markets train in simulators that decide who bears losses beyond capital. An energy-trading engine's protocol shortcuts ranked first a controller reporting +162% to +795% in all six runs while booking -1,822% to -3,655%: the engine floored cash at zero and forgave the rest, mostly unpaid costs. Such rules are options agents learn to exercise: floor-trained agents reported +38% to +148% while booking -15% to -57% in placebo markets, and in an unmodified public simulator with leverage and a linear reward, proximal policy optimization took maximal leverage at 89% of probes and went bust in 61% of episodes (3% and 2% with bankruptcy losses charged). The contribution in artificial intelligence is reading a simulator's accounting from its reward. Along the market input, on a clone, the reward is the payoff diagram of that option book: kinks locate floors and caps, tails give exposure, and booked profit follows without ledger, formula or account structure, given the own cost. We prove when this is exact, where it cannot be, and that error falls with the input's range, not the query count. A sign-reversed twin, its two-point case, brackets booked profit and yields rewards that kept 99% to 115% of the ledger-trained edge with losses floored per account or position, where a 5% balance ruin penalty failed. On real engine clones, the reading recovered booked return within 0.0001 points in ten runs; twin estimates erred by 9 to 1,410. The engineering application is validating RL simulators for electricity trading.

## Status
Accompanies a manuscript in preparation for submission to *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*. Every registered study has its protocol, hashes, append-only run log, results and verdicts in its folder; `python tools/verify_studies.py` re-checks the registered hashes, re-runs the unit tests of the twin rewards and re-checks the numbers of the abstract and of the registered-study tables against the stored results (it does not re-train agents). Dated errata accompany the hashed protocols of Studies T, W, X, Z and EXT, and Study RS's README discloses its registration defect. Exploratory work, including every failure, is kept in the `exploratory_*` folders: Study S's development is in `exploratory_claude_2026-10-16/nfl_training/` (`PLAN.md`, `NFL_THEORY.md`); the censored-reward theory, its exact checks and the Study T pilots are in `exploratory_claude_2026-10-31/`.
